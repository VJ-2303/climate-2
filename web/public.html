What This Means For Your Daily Routine

⚡ Outdoor Labor & Construction

If WBGT exceeds 30°C, take a mandatory 15-minute shaded rest for every 45 minutes of heavy manual work. Cease unshaded rooftop tasks between 11:30 AM and 3:30 PM.

🏠 Metal-Roofed Homes

Corrugated tin sheets can reach 48°C surface skin temperatures. Create cross-ventilation by propping open opposite openings. Hang wet fabric across doorways to enable evaporative cooling.

💧 Hydration Standards

Do not wait until feeling thirsty. Consume at least 250ml of clean drinking water every hour. Avoid high-sugar carbonated drinks which accelerate physiological dehydration.

👶 Vulnerable Family Members

Infants, pregnant mothers, and elderly neighbors cannot regulate core body temperature efficiently. Check on them during peak afternoon heat hours (1 PM – 4 PM).
