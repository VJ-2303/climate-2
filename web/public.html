Health & Protection Actions
  • Hydrate actively: Drink ORS or water every 45–60 minutes, even if you do not feel thirsty.
  • Use shaded corridors: Avoid direct asphalt streets and dense built-up walkways during midday sun (11 AM – 3:30 PM).
  • Flush interior heat: Open opposing windows for cross-breeze to remove radiated indoor heat.
  • Protect vulnerable residents: Keep elders, infants, and pregnant family members in cool, ventilated rooms.
